## 3. Community Fair Use (Allowed Without Permission)

You may use the name **NFTBAN** **to truthfully describe**:

- Compatibility with or reference to the project (e.g., "tool for use with nftban")
- Contributions or community editions based on the **MPL-2.0-licensed Core**
- Non-commercial educational or research material that discusses NFTBAN
- Technical documentation, blog posts, or tutorials about NFTBAN

When using the name in this way, you must:

- Clearly state that your project is **not affiliated with or endorsed by NFTBAN**
- Include a visible attribution such as:

  > "NFTBAN is a trademark of Antonios Voulvoulis. Used with permission for descriptive purposes only."

## 4. Prohibited Uses (Require Written Permission)

You **may not**, without written permission:

- Use the name or logo to **brand or market** your own fork or distribution
- Present your version as "official", "certified", or "partnered" with NFTBAN

- Modify or remix the official logos or wordmarks
- Register domain names, social accounts, or services using the NFTBAN name in a confusing way
