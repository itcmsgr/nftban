Notes & tips

The auto-apply path unit watches:

/etc/nftban/config/nftban-configuration-ipv4-blacklist_ips.conf.local
/etc/nftban/config/nftban-configuration-ipv6-blacklist_ips.conf.local
/etc/nftban/config/nftban-configuration-user-whitelist_ips.conf.local (so whitelist edits also trigger reload)


The timer runs hourly (OnCalendar=hourly). Change it (e.g. every 15′):
INI# /etc/systemd/system/nftban.timer[Timer]OnCalendar=*:0/15Εμφάνιση περισσότερων γραμμών
Then: systemctl daemon-reload && systemctl restart nftban.timer
The script refuses to ban your current login IP.
Reload only applies if /etc/nftables.conf passes nft -c; otherwise changes remain on disk (safe).
