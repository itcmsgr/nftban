## 1. Permitted Fair Use

You **may**:
- Use the **word “nftban”** descriptively to reference the open‑source project (e.g., “Compatible with nftban”, “Based on nftban”).  
- Use the **official logo** to show compatibility or community support **in non‑commercial contexts**, following `/branding/README.md`.

When attribution is needed, include:
> “nftban is a trademark of the NFTBAN Project / Antonios Voulvoulis.  
> This work is not affiliated with or endorsed by the nftban project.”

---

## 2. Restricted Uses

You **may not**:
- Use “nftban” or its marks in ways implying endorsement, affiliation, or official status without written permission.  
- Register confusingly similar names (e.g., `nftban‑enterprise`, `nftban‑official`).  
- Modify or merge the logo with another mark.  
- Use the marks in misleading, defamatory, or illegal contexts.

For commercial use or co‑branding, contact **legal@nftban.com**.

---

## 3. Forks and Derivatives

Forks under MPL‑2.0 are encouraged. However:
- They must use distinct names and branding.  
- They must not suggest official status or partnership.  
- You may describe forks as “a fork of nftban” or “based on nftban”.
